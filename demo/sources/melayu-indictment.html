TH0041 · The prosecutor supplies a general alleged separatist/terrorist-movement background.

Derived DOCX body paragraph 41 (includes table-cell paragraphs; not an official page or paragraph number)

Supplied English prosecution plaint; source-attributed preparation by Codex, pending human review

	1. Many years prior to the incident, a group consisting of more than five persons, who concealed their unlawful methods and purposes, jointly formed a terrorist group named “Pattani State Independence Movement”. The group’s purpose was to gain independence from the Thai Kingdom, that is, forming an independent state consisting of Pattani Province, Yala Province, Narathiwat Province, and some districts of Songkhla Province, called “the Pattani State or Pattani Darussalam”. The act, which is a criminal offence liable to harsh penalties starting from a prison term of one year, involved an act of violence or any act to cause a danger to life or a serious harm to any person’s body or freedom, serious and continuous acts of terrorism in various forms, and collecting forces or arms. The act also involved spreading propaganda among, instigating, and inciting Muslim citizens residing in the Southern Border Provinces and some districts of Songkhla Province to believe that the said area, historically belonged to the Muslim Malays, was invaded and conquered by Thailand (Siam) and was governed without regards to the Muslim citizens, or even oppressed them. Thai authorities treated the Muslims unfairly and discriminatorily. Consequently, some Muslim citizens who had heard the propaganda and instigating words were convinced and developed hatred against the Buddhist citizens, Thai authorities, and Thai government, and, in turn, joined the said movement. The group proceeded with the separation according to the movement’s purposes, persuaded relatives and friends to join the movement as members or allies, dispatched religious teachers or Ustaz to kindergartens called “tadika” and private religious schools (pondok) in order to instill the ideas of separatism and hatred against Thai authorities and government among children and youth and pondok pupils. The students were trained in discipline to obey the Ustaz. The group also expanded to the secondary education students in the pondoks and other schools, indoctrinating them with the idea of Pattani State. The movement followed a clear division of responsibilities, consisting of local politics group, funding group, religious organization group, terrorism group, and state authorities group. The movement secretly trained their members in the use of arms, military skills, and guerrilla tactics in order to commit acts of terrorism and secede themselves. The movement received funding through donation from affluent members and allies, as well as overseas allies, allowing the movement to purchase and accumulate arms. A portion of arms was acquired by plundering from the police, the military, and the administrative agencies. When considerable number of forces and arms was mobilized, the movement established armed forces in order to commit serious and continuous acts of terrorism in various locations in the Southern Border Provinces and some districts of Songkhla Province with no fear of the law. The movement challenged the authority of the state, used violence to threaten and kill officials from various agencies and local resident, set fire to schools, and planted bombs at government offices, shops, and communities, with the objectives to intimidate Thai government and create turmoil and fear among the people. The act was considered offences of insurrection, terrorism, secret society, and criminal association. The offences were broadly known to continue until the present moment and only became increasingly serious.
