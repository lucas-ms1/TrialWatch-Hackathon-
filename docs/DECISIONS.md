## October 9 latest clarification: one interface for strategy and collective-action signals

The final product format is **an interface integrating advocacy strategy with collective-action identification and early-warning candidates from public news and social-media evidence**. Collective action is now part of the connected workflow, superseding its earlier description as a secondary component. The intended purpose is to help advocates recognize a shared issue early, increase its visibility, support related cases and strengthen community agency.

The user's example is several cases involving arrests during protests in a similar area. The proposed sequence is: propose an explainable cluster of potentially related cases; inspect public NGO/civil-society, news and permitted social-media evidence; assess whether it documents planned or ongoing collective action; find comparable cases and movements; compare advocacy approaches and relevant organizations, institutions and moments; then prepare a human-reviewed strategy judgment and, where supported, a conditional outcome estimate. A cluster may justify investigation without establishing that a coordinated movement exists. The example illustrates a workflow, not a decision to replace the accepted notice/response evidence drill with a new real dataset.

Cluster review must distinguish reports, cases, people and arrest events, show the proposed time/geography/issue connections, and retain duplicate links, uncertain identities, contradictory evidence and common upstream sources. NGO or civil-society material supplies an attributed perspective; its presence alone does not establish independent corroboration. A newly observed cluster, an announced action, reported activity underway and a forecast of future action are different outputs. Early-warning candidates require human review and are not findings of violations or validated predictions. Public organization/event-level context is in scope; private participant tracking is not.

Outcome estimation remains an explicitly requested intended capability. Define the outcome and horizon before estimating; separate observed results, evidence of contribution and conditional forecasts. Neither chronological order nor a forecast conditional on a tactic establishes its causal effect. The specific practitioner, first operational task, owners, forecast outcome/horizon and implementation priority remain open. Interface format is settled; exports and views should be chosen for those tasks.
