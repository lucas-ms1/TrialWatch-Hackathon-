# Candidate-pattern review worksheet

Blank preparation form, October 9, 2026. Copy it into an ignored local research folder for each possible pattern. It is a way to check an idea, not a chosen dataset, audience, final output or finding. It can later support a brief, evidence map or investigation checklist. No pattern analysis has been run by creating this form.

Repetition means a feature recurs in this material. A change claim additionally needs comparable periods, case/event linkage and source coverage. Neither establishes a population trend, escalation or a forecast. The team has not established forecasting ability.

A distinct publisher or URL does not establish independence. “Separate reporting basis documented” records a bounded provenance judgment about this claim; it does not certify the claim or every statement in the source. Dossiers are analyst groupings and are not automatically unique cases. Keep uncertain matches visible instead of merging them.

For each observation, record only the features supported by the passage. Leave absent or ambiguous features unknown. A shared feature is a connection to investigate, not proof of a shared cause.

## 3. What weakens the interpretation?

- **Contradictory evidence / exceptions / uncertain links:**
- **Alternative explanations:** include repeated upstream reporting, changes in source coverage, ordinary procedure or other relevant explanations:
- **Coverage and missingness:** what was searched, accessible, excluded or unavailable; changes between periods; what “not found” means here:
- **If suggesting change:** comparable periods and search method; event-date coverage; identity/deduplication basis; denominator if claiming a rate; reasons comparison may be misleading:
- **Optional batch inventory:** observation rows, reports, provisionally linked events and reviewer-confirmed proceedings, each separately labeled; unresolved IDs listed:

Do not turn reporting volume into incident counts. Counts describe the bounded collection and its recorded linkage judgments, not prevalence, reach, independent corroboration or advocacy effectiveness. Unknown is not zero.

An AI proposal is not a human decision. Even a retained candidate is a reason to investigate, not an automated finding of abuse, legal violation or guilt.

**This worksheet has no automatic stale-evidence warning.** Before sharing or relying on it, manually recheck every dependency against current evidence and source-lineage judgments. Record corrections, revise or withdraw the candidate as needed, and preserve its earlier version. The app's own saved analyses retain snapshots and show later review changes; that behavior does not automatically extend to this separate document.

Filled forms, real excerpts and review records stay in ignored local research storage, outside the synthetic public package. This blank form does not authorize publication, submission, monitoring or contact with anyone.
