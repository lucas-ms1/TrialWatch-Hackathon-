# Connected rights assessment and advocacy decision support

October 9 clarification supersedes the rights-only primary framing. The evidence-backed fair-trial/expression index now connects to dated case/cluster context, alternative advocacy options, a reasoned intervention recommendation, human disposition and later follow-up. Track 3: Advocacy and Impact remains selected; legal evaluation overlaps Track 2 without an approved track change. This is a hackathon prototype, not an official TrialWatch assessment or action authority.

Core flow: supplied evidence → versioned rights profile → scoped review → source-backed context → alternative strategies → proposed whether/how/when intervention → accept/revise/reject/defer → index/cited export. Recommendations snapshot exact dependencies and become stale when those change. Legal concern, urgency, opportunity, feasibility, harm and uncertainty remain separate.

The framework covers thirteen core fair-trial dimensions, three conditional safeguards and four expression dimensions. Old assessments retain original definitions; no combined counsel score is distributed to new dimensions. Due process is an umbrella, not an extra score. Article 9 detention context remains separately identifiable. No composite or calibrated probability is produced.

The bounded implementation prioritizes one evidence-to-pending-proposal walkthrough, with a portable synthetic fallback and separately local retained real evidence. Real evidence is reused within the already consumed research ceiling; no new operational collection. The [pending AI draft path](AI_DRAFTING.md) imports completed artifacts and exposes an optional one-attempt authenticated CLI route. Structured/manual inputs and explicit deterministic worksheets remain usable without model access. Twenty real-drill legal mappings stay unassigned; source preparation is separate from human legal and advocacy judgments.
