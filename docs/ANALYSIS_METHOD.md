# Connected analysis method and limits

Use [RIGHTS_RUBRIC](RIGHTS_RUBRIC.md) for versioned per-right concern and [INTERVENTION_METHOD](INTERVENTION_METHOD.md) for context/options/decision factors. The rights profile is the foundation; legal concern is not a weighted intervention score or an action trigger. Missing evidence does not become zero or confident advice. Duplicate reports do not automatically raise concern, confidence or opportunity.

Inspect attribution, exact passages, competing accounts, applicability/stage/exceptions, language/translation, identity, source dependence and distinct date roles. Publicity evaluates access to hearings/judgments and justified exceptions; government/news volume and document availability belong in completeness. Country indicators remain separate contextual values.

Contextual claims need dates, uncertainty and freshness. An institutional opportunity requires issue-specific position, authority, route and time window; party labels/deadlines alone are insufficient. Compare alternatives and their benefits/harms/prerequisites. Unknown consent/preferences stays unknown. Scope legal and recommendation reviews separately; useful verification need not wait for whole-case approval.

Assessments, contexts and proposals retain versions/snapshots. Corrections, reassessment and context revisions flag dependent recommendations stale, without overwriting old human dispositions. Earlier counsel/preparation and tribunal definitions remain preserved; new/split dimensions start unassessed. Outcome follow-up separates planning, action, outcome and conditional scenario; observed sequence does not prove contribution or effectiveness.

Cases/documents/defendants/arrest events/clusters remain distinct units. Corpus counts are not national incidence, systemic misconduct or a movement finding. Comparable histories must show material differences and contrary/unresolved examples. Published expert conclusions already read are attributed evidence, not independent evaluation labels. Software tests do not validate legal accuracy or advocacy usefulness. Probability development needs independent labels, leakage prevention, evaluation and calibration, with no estimate currently implemented.

[The previous method](history/ANALYSIS_METHOD.before-connected-intervention-2026-10-09.md) and frozen historical outputs remain preserved. No research ceiling reset, monitoring, outreach or execution is authorized.
