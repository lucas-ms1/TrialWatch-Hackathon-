## Recommendation based on this check

Demonstrate a **candidate recurring procedural feature plus documented advocacy responses**, across several distinct case/event groups. Show evidence that supports it, an apparently corroborating report with shared or unknown lineage, and a contrary record or explicit gap. End with why a person would investigate, narrow or reject the candidate.

The team should inspect a few permitted documents from two or three material types before selecting the feature and histories. A possible ceiling is 3–5 histories and 8–12 narrow observations. It is a scope suggestion, not a required dataset, a two-case comparison or a claim that enough suitable real material already exists. If the trial finds no defensible pattern, demonstrate that outcome honestly or use a clearly fictional proof of concept.

Compare outputs by the task: a brief for a briefing paragraph; an evidence table for comparing support and challenges; a timeline for checking sequence. Do not build all three before seeing which helps. A region-level increase or forecast is a poor first demonstration because stable collection coverage, event deduplication and time-separated outcome evaluation are missing.
