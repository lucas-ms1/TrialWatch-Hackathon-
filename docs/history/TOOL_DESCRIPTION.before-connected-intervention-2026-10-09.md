# Intended use

TrialWatch Rights Index is a local hackathon prototype for advocates comparing fair-trial and freedom-of-expression case evidence. It provides separate, explainable per-right concern profiles with cited support, contrary evidence, missing facts and exact human-review scope, then exports the evidence and methodology for investigation and advocacy prioritization.

Track 3: Advocacy and Impact remains the team's framing. Fact-to-standard assessment overlaps Track 2; no approved track change is asserted. The prototype is not an official TrialWatch assessment, expert certification or calibrated probability. Published TrialWatch grades stay attributed separately.

The application uses structured/manual intake and deterministic anchored rules. No fresh AI extraction, source discovery, monitoring or forecasting is integrated. Related cases and context support comparison; selected corpus frequencies do not establish national incidence. Synthetic defaults are fictional. Real evidence and local review databases are excluded from the shared package.

Intended output is a reviewed investigation aid, with qualified human legal review and evidence verification required before relying on conclusions. No operational adoption, measured usefulness or advocacy impact has been established.
