**Track 3: Advocacy and Impact | Philippines | Working plan, October 9**
The demonstration, audience and owners remain open. This replaces the earlier two-history plan.

**The idea.** Connect scattered case information with documented advocacy and public responses. Help an advocate investigate recurring actions, affected groups, institutions, legal provisions, places and procedural developments. Show supporting passages, contradictions and gaps so a person can decide what needs checking and what response to consider.

**Explore first (30–45 minutes).** Check what material we have, what we may use and what questions it can answer. We have a fictional prototype, historical reporting and organizer resource pointers; unrestricted database access is unverified. Inspect a few permitted records across different histories. What repeats? Which reports repeat one account? What advocacy is documented? Do not default to journalist arrests or the previously proposed pair.

**Recommendation, not a team decision:** demonstrate one possible recurring procedural feature, documented advocacy responses, and a contrary example or explicit gap. Roughly 3–5 histories and 8–12 short observations is a possible ceiling, not a quota. Choose the feature after inspecting evidence; use a clearly fictional example if needed. A possible pattern does not establish a trend or forecast.
