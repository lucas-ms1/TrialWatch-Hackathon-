# TrialWatch Philippines: patterns across cases and advocacy responses

The project aims to help advocates connect scattered legal-case information with documented public and advocacy responses. Candidate connections may involve actions, affected groups, institutions, legal provisions, locations or procedural developments. The intended endpoint is a traceable account of a possible issue, supporting and contradictory evidence, relevant documented actors and the next question to investigate. The first use case, audience and final output remain open.

The implemented baseline is a local, manual campaign-comparison application: an analyst reviews source-attributed observations, preserves corrections, codes evidence, compares several timelines and exports editable Markdown/JSON with excerpts, dates, unknowns and alternative explanations. Saved analyses retain evidence snapshots and show later review changes.

Blank pattern-review worksheets and an optional existing-model proposal prompt prepare the broader workflow outside the app. AI extraction, automatic linkage, pattern discovery, change detection and forecasting are not implemented. Worksheets require manual evidence rechecking. Finding a possible pattern is different from detecting a change under stable collection coverage or predicting future developments.

Three fictional dossiers demonstrate software behavior; the historical research and frozen AI experiment remain separate and excluded from the public package. An existing model with examples and human review is the recommended first AI trial; training is optional. Practitioner usefulness and real-world impact remain unmeasured. No new collection, model execution, monitoring or outreach runs. Original code/docs and synthetic fixtures are MIT licensed; third-party source rights remain separate.
