# Intervention decision worksheet v1

October 9, 2026. This original hackathon method connects a source-backed rights profile to a proposed advocacy decision. It is not an official TrialWatch mandate, legal finding, probability forecast or an instruction to execute advocacy. The rights index remains the evidence foundation; Track 3 frames prioritization/comparison, with Track 2 overlap.

## Distinct components

| Component | Meaning and required distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Legal concern | Per-dimension ordinal assessment, with exact rubric/evidence revision. It never automatically chooses an action. |
| Urgency | Evidenced procedural or practical time pressure; a deadline alone does not establish benefit. |
| Opportunity | Issue-specific position, actor authority and available route, supported by dated passages. Party identity or ideology alone cannot establish receptiveness. |
| Feasibility | Practical access, capacity, partner availability and prerequisites. An institution's name does not prove access or authority. |
| Potential harm | Exposure, retaliation, interference, unwanted attention and other consequences, including contrary evidence. Unknown risks are unresolved. |
| Uncertainty | Source dependence, missing records, identity, translations, freshness and unknown preferences/consent; separate from concern severity. |

For institutional/political changes, show the whole chain: institutional change → issue-specific position → authority over a relevant decision → available advocacy route → time window. An illustrative partisan example is not a verified premise or a universal mechanism.

## Source-backed context

A context record is dated and tied to exact cases or a reviewed candidate cluster. Each factual proposition retains a source URL/publisher/title, supplied source text, exact excerpt/locator, relevant date, attribution, uncertainty and freshness note. Unknown dates/preferences remain explicit. Historical positions never silently become current. The software checks excerpt presence and ID relationships, not authenticity or substantive support.

Record procedural stage/hearings/appeals/deadlines; issue/affected groups; public organizations and documented advocacy; attention/salience; institutional authority/routes; government positions/policy changes; comparable histories/legal opportunities; practical capacity; potential harms; and properly available stated preferences. A sparse record can still support targeted verification, with other components explicitly unknown. Country-year indicators remain contextual and cannot alter case scores.

Clusters are candidate analytical groupings. Explain time/place/issue membership, reasons for including each case, differences, uncertain identity and the exact human membership review. Cases, reports, defendants and arrest events remain different units. Public organization/event context is in scope; private participant tracking is excluded. Shared geography is not a coordinated movement or systemic finding.

## Alternatives and deterministic proposal

Each option identifies objective/beneficiary, route/actor/authority, source-backed opportunity and timing, support/contrary passages, prerequisites, unresolved questions, practical feasibility, possible benefits/harms, alternatives including deferral, next step and reconsideration trigger. The worksheet displays which factors support or block the proposed disposition. No weighted intervention score, article-count heuristic, party heuristic or legal-score threshold is used.

| Proposal disposition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Intervene now | Specify route, actor and timing with evidenced prerequisites. It remains a proposal for review, not an executed action. |
| Prepare / targeted verification | A concrete bounded next check can be worthwhile despite pending legal/context judgments. Explain what must be resolved first. |
| Referral / coordination | Appropriate partner/mechanism and relevant jurisdiction/access prerequisites are evidenced; actual referral is not sent. |
| Defer specified intervention | Name route, condition/time and useful interim work. Deferring publicity is not doing nothing. |
| Against specified intervention at present | Explain adverse evidence/harms or lack of fit for that route; do not infer this from missing data. |
| Insufficient basis | No supported recommendation is available; state the information needed. This is not advice against intervention. |

A proposal snapshots exact rights assessment revisions, their original standards/evidence and review scope, context revision/passages, options, source cutoff, author/origin and generation date. Pending analysis retains its status; no whole-case approval gate applies. Changed evidence, reassessment or material context invalidates fresh reliance and review but never overwrites the old snapshot or human decision.

Human reviewers accept, revise, reject or defer with reasons and exact recommendation/right-dimension/context-evidence scope. Human disposition and generated disposition are separate fields. Revise records a separately attributed revised disposition and revision note against the immutable proposal. It does not generate or overwrite a proposal. Changes to options or their factual basis require a new proposal and fresh review. Authority to save a prototype decision is not authority to act for TrialWatch or affected people.

## Comparable histories and outcomes

Analogue records explain material similarities/differences in law, institutions, stage and context; document the earlier action, observed outcome and period; identify contrary examples/unresolved outcomes; and distinguish contribution evidence from chronological sequence. Most-reported tactics are not automatically best. Current supporting panel is supplied context, not automatic precedent or movement discovery.

Plans remain in options and next steps; no separate planned-action follow-up kind is implemented. The four saved follow-up kinds are observed action, observed outcome, conditional scenario and unknown outcome. Observed claims need source passages; an action plan is not completion. Conditional scenarios identify assumptions and reconsideration conditions without numerical likelihood. No causal effect, success probability or effectiveness result is claimed. Defensible probability work requires independent labels, leakage prevention, held-out evaluation and calibration described in [RIGHTS_RUBRIC](RIGHTS_RUBRIC.md).

## Fictional strategy generation

The Mara Vale worked example adds a browser-only route assembler, available in the local app and the published read-only demo. It uses the fictional rights issues and source-backed context to generate preparation, counsel-remedy, private-coordination and specified-publicity options. Each route then passes through separate opportunity, authority, timing, capacity, harm and preference checks matching the existing worksheet decision rules. Supporting and competing quotations remain accessible.

Scenario updates are authored fictional assumptions shown separately from the saved context: a limited counsel mandate/capacity and private safeguards; requested confidential clinic coordination; or opposition to named publicity. Consent to one private route does not supply consent to publicity or filing. The generator rejects stale or changed source versions rather than attaching those assumptions to later records. All previews and legal assessments remain pending human review; downloading a preview neither saves nor executes a proposal. This illustrative template system does not generate strategies for other cases or make model requests.

## Demonstration and limits

The synthetic walkthrough uses a documented concern, competing delivery account, fictional procedural opportunity, two options (bounded legal-support/verification and deferral of public advocacy), a reasoned proposal and a clearly labelled simulated human decision. A later correction flags it stale. The rehearsal also exercises insufficient basis and justified deferral. No actual legal review, organizational consent, outreach or advocacy occurs. Verify actual behavior in [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md), then assign qualified review/usefulness testing separately.
