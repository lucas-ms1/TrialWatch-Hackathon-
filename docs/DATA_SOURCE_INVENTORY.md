# Practical data-source inventory

Verified October 9, 2026 during the local rights-index pivot. This is a resource/access inventory, not a populated case database. Public resources need document-by-document provenance and human review. The pilot does not depend on an internal TrialWatch database, a paid service, a new account, or permission obtained through outreach.

Checks below distinguish a working public page, a readable document, a search-index preview, and a failed retrieval. A failed tool request is not proof that a resource is unavailable to an ordinary user. No authentication, access restriction or paywall was bypassed. Actual case notes, short passage captures, search decisions and the new pilot seed stay in ignored `outputs/rights_pivot_20261009/research/`. The existing v2 corpus and review database remain untouched.

## Intake priorities

1. Public national judgment/procedural documents and source-attributed trial observations for the actual case.
2. Special Procedures letters **and** government replies; WGAD opinions or Committee views with their procedural status.
3. Detailed TrialWatch reports, retaining their published expert conclusions separately from our provisional rights profiles.
4. NGO reporting/news for missing facts, competing accounts and context; independent provenance must be established rather than assumed.
5. Country-year context displayed separately; it cannot change an individual case score.

Documents, proceedings, defendants and arrest events have different identifiers. A report can cover several proceedings; several reports can repeat one account. Counts in the index cover its selected corpus, not national incidence or the publisher's entire population.

## Country context selection

Display only fields tied to the product's two rights families. The selected **V-Dem v16** context candidates are `v2juhcind` (high court independence), `v2juncind` (lower court independence), and `v2x_freexp_altinf` (expression/alternative information). The [codebook](https://www.v-dem.net/documents/70/codebook_v16.pdf) identifies the judicial variables in §§3.8.1.8–9 and expression index in §2.2.3. Include estimate type, units, bounds, release, country/year and coverage; transformed latent estimates are not the expert coders' raw ordinal labels. Access to justice can be added only with a stated relevance reason.

For **Freedom in the World**, use D1 (media), D4 (personal expression), F1 (judiciary) and F2 (due process). D3 (academic expression) is relevant when case material warrants it. The annual report's overall democracy/political-rights total is broader than this index and should not substitute for these questions. The [2026 Philippines](https://freedomhouse.org/country/philippines/freedom-world/2026) and [Thailand](https://freedomhouse.org/country/thailand/freedom-world/2026) pages are usable context leads, not October 2026 case-status evidence. No real country values are embedded in the shareable synthetic demo.

Changing or removing country context must leave case dimensions unchanged. A country-level expert estimate and a cited individual-case proposition answer different questions. Cross-country differences in document publication, trial access, language and NGO attention affect the collected sample.

## Packet link audit and scope decisions

PDF page 4's printed Indonesia footnote names South Jakarta, while its hyperlink goes to a Lubuk Basung page; its printed Thailand footnote repeats an Indonesian address while the annotation correctly goes to the COJ tracker. Neither Indonesian route yielded a usable document in this pass. Zimbabwe's [schedule link](https://jsccallcentre.org.zw/course-list/) timed out and [ZimLII](https://zimlii.org/) returned 403. Both remain unverified access leads, outside the initial jurisdictions. Page 7's African Court label points to the IHRDA analyser; the official African Court portal is listed above. Uwazi is [documentation software](https://uwazi.io/), not a promised source corpus; its software license does not license data held in a Uwazi instance.

The case-document pass retained **two additional detailed reports** for a three-history Philippines/Thailand working sample, reusing the prior Philippine history by reference. Direct original PDF download attempts failed and are logged; public-reader passage inspection succeeded. Search previews for alternative cases/documents were excluded once these reports supported the workflow. No new full original court order or underlying Thai monitor notes/translated judgments were acquired. Published expert grades are attributed external assessments. They must be masked from any future independent evaluation input, and this extraction pass supplies no test of legal accuracy, scoring calibration or national prevalence.

Read [the rubric](RIGHTS_RUBRIC.md) before selecting an applicable right and [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) before packaging. The explicit public allowlist excludes all populated source notes, passage captures, runtime state, organizer documents and real pilot seeds.
