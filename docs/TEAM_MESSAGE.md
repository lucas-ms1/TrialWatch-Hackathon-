# Group update — ready to send

Prepared October 9, 2026. This message has not been sent to anyone. The [team start page](TEAM_START_HERE.md) and [sprint plan](SPRINT_PLAN.md) contain the details.

We're building a tool to help advocates connect related cases, understand public responses and collective action, compare advocacy approaches, and decide what to investigate or do next. We are starting with the Philippines and building our evidence from public documents, without relying on a TrialWatch database.

We now have a basic working app for inspecting sources, recording reviews, comparing timelines and producing summaries with citations. AI helped prepare 20 evidence points from eight public documents covering three provisional case histories, plus five suggested connections. These are 20 observations, not 20 separate cases.

We've completed the first guided human review of one connection in the Abra history. Four evidence points were selected, the review and analysis were saved, the summary was exported, and the saved analysis was confirmed after reopening. We accepted what the sources report as a sequence of events. The original court order and some identity questions remain unchecked, and we have not established that advocacy caused the outcome.

The next job is to bring the pieces together. The app does not yet automatically discover related cases, monitor movements or predict outcomes. The newer clickable interface shows the intended direction, while the working app still relies on manual review. We also need to fix wording that still shows old preparation labels after a review has been saved.
