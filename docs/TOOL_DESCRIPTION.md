# TrialWatch Philippines Campaign Evidence Comparison

TrialWatch Philippines Campaign Evidence Comparison is a Track 3 prototype for advocacy analysts examining how documented social-media activity, international attention and human-rights campaigns relate in time to reported legal or policy developments.

An analyst inspects cited statements, preserves gaps and corrections, classifies observations, and records an interpretation with alternative explanations. The comparison view and editable Markdown/JSON memo place campaign activity beside reported outcomes, retaining excerpts, dates, provenance and evidence revisions. Later corrections flag saved analyses for refresh. Missing evidence remains unknown; the tool does not infer causation, rank campaigns or treat source counts as audience reach.

The application runs locally with Python's standard library and SQLite. Its three fictional campaign dossiers and offline fallback demonstrate the workflow without accounts, internet, API keys or new model calls. Earlier AI-assisted research and coding are disclosed; historical triage results do not measure campaign effectiveness. The frozen real research corpus and human feedback remain separate and excluded from the public package.

The intended contribution is a clearer evidence base for comparing campaigns and identifying what must be researched before drawing advocacy lessons. Practitioner usefulness, time savings and real-world impact remain unmeasured. No automated collection, outreach, current-status verification or causal analysis runs. The project uses pre-event preparation under the user's assumption of permission; no organizer endorsement or operational adoption is claimed.
