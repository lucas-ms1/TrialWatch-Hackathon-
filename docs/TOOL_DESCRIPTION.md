# Intended use — TrialWatch Rights and Intervention Desk

A local hackathon prototype for advocates and human reviewers to inspect fair-trial/expression evidence, compare provisional per-right profiles, examine dated case/cluster context and consider alternative advocacy routes. It proposes whether/how/when intervention or targeted verification may be appropriate, records exact human dispositions and exports cited decision snapshots. Track 3 advocacy prioritization/comparison remains the framing; legal analysis overlaps Track 2 without an approved change.

Supplied passages and competing accounts support explicit analyst mappings. The pending-draft view can reuse a bounded saved model output with exact hashes, citations, stage questions, gaps and alternatives; an optional authenticated local CLI route is separately bounded and labels actual request results. It never assigns concern scores or approves sources. Deterministic ordinal concern rules and intervention worksheets keep legal concern, urgency, opportunity, feasibility, harm and uncertainty separate. There is no composite percentage, calibrated probability, official TrialWatch assessment or automated organizational action. Context and candidate links require attribution, dates, uncertainty and freshness; no private participant tracking.

Recommendations can depend on pending analysis with that status visible. Human review is scoped to listed dimensions, passages and recommendation revisions, not whole-case authentication. Changed dependencies flag stale decisions while preserving original snapshots/history. Plans remain in options and next steps; follow-up records observed actions, observed outcomes, conditional scenarios or unknown outcomes. Chronology is not causation.

Synthetic fixtures demonstrate software only. Real evidence/recommendations stay ignored locally and need qualified legal/context review. Source validity, legal accuracy, advocacy usefulness/effectiveness and probability calibration are not established by tests. No monitoring, outreach, spending, publication or submission is performed.
